HOW THE HEARTBEAT WORKS (frontend -> Argus)

Every 10 seconds, the Student page sends POST /api/snapshot with
event: "heartbeat", the current code, and an "activity" object with 4 numbers.


THE 4 NUMBERS

1. seconds_since_last_key
   A stopwatch: seconds since the kid last pressed a key.
   Resets to 0 only when the kid presses a key.
   Otherwise it keeps growing across heartbeats (10, 20, 30 ...).

2. seconds_since_cursor_move
   A stopwatch: seconds since the cursor last moved
   (typing, clicking, or arrow keys all move it).
   Resets to 0 only when the kid types, clicks, or moves the cursor.

3. keys_typed
   Letters, numbers, symbols, space, Enter and Tab pressed
   since the PREVIOUS heartbeat.
   Resets to 0 after every heartbeat.

4. keys_deleted
   Backspace and Delete pressed since the PREVIOUS heartbeat.
   Resets to 0 after every heartbeat.

Not counted: shortcuts (like Ctrl+Enter to run) and modifier keys alone (like Shift).

A snapshot with event: "run" is also sent on every Run, with code, output and error.


HOW ARGUS SHOULD READ IT (values in config.STUCK_RULES)

Idle:
  Stuck when BOTH seconds_since_last_key AND seconds_since_cursor_move
  are 60 or more.
  A kid who clicks around reading their code is not idle.

Typing and deleting a lot:
  Add up keys_typed and keys_deleted over the LAST 3 HEARTBEATS (about 30 s).
  Stuck when deleted / typed is 0.7 or more
  AND the total keys (typed + deleted) are at least 20.
  Example: typed 6 + 12 + 8 = 26, deleted 0 + 10 + 8 = 18.
           18 / 26 = 69%  -> not stuck yet.

Then the usual rules apply:
  - The first idle hint is level 1.
  - 45-second cooldown between hints.
  - After level 3, the ship turns red for the teacher.
